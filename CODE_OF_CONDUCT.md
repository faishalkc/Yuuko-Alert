# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Yuuko Alert project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to foster an environment where people can participate openly and respectfully, and where disagreements are handled constructively.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate of others.
- Welcoming constructive feedback and different perspectives.
- Giving feedback in a helpful and respectful manner.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or community setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication.

It also applies when someone is representing the project or its community in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainer through GitHub.

For sensitive or security-related matters, do not post details publicly. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the project's security reporting guidance.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible. The maintainer will handle reports at their discretion and may take action to protect the project community.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and context of the behavior, responses may include:

- A reminder or request to stop.
- A warning.
- Temporary restriction from project spaces.
- Removal of comments, contributions, or access.
- A permanent ban from project spaces.

The maintainer may take any action considered appropriate to maintain a respectful environment.
